FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Call us any time.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead guarantees your slot and reduces waiting time. Emergencies are seen immediately, 24/7.

Which insurance providers do you accept?

We work with most major insurers and TPAs and offer cashless treatment. Contact our front desk with your policy details to confirm.

How will I receive my test reports?

Reports are shared securely via SMS/email link, and printed copies are available at the centre.

Do you offer online consultations?

Yes. Many of our specialists offer video consultations for follow-ups and second opinions.
